#### **What numpy and pandas are**

##### **• NumPy is for fast math on large lists of numbers. Think of it as a better, faster list for calculations.**

##### **• Pandas is for tables of data, like a spreadsheet in code. You load a CSV file and can then filter rows, calculate averages, and clean data in a line or two.**

##### **• Why they matter: ML and AI work is mostly data in tables and arrays. Libraries like scikit-learn expect numpy and pandas input, so you can't skip them.**

##### **The path to ML is core Python, then pandas and numpy, then charts, then a basic model. A first ML model fits well after the hackathon, or as a small stretch goal during it.**
