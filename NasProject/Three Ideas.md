##### **🌍 1. AI that helps protect Earth**

##### 

##### 

##### **Concept: EarthWatch AI**

##### 

##### **Imagine a tool that helps people understand environmental changes using satellite data.**

##### 

##### **Potential features:**

##### **- Analyze satellite imagery or environmental datasets.**

##### **- Visualize changes in vegetation or land cover.**

##### **- Use AI to identify patterns in the data.**

##### **- Explain the findings through graphs and summaries.**

##### **Possible technologies: Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and machine learning libraries.**

##### 

##### **The challenge would be ensuring the data actually supports the conclusions you draw. You shouldn't claim to predict disasters reliably without validating the model.**







##### **🪐 2. AI that helps us explore space**

##### 

##### **Concept: Exoplanet Explorer**

##### 

##### **Exoplanets are planets that orbit stars outside our solar system.**

##### 

##### **You could create a Python tool that:**

##### **- Loads real astronomical data.**

##### **- Visualizes how a star's brightness changes over time.**

##### **- Helps users understand how scientists detect possible planets.**

##### **- Uses basic machine learning to classify data, if appropriate.**

##### **This would be a fantastic learning project because it combines astronomy, data analysis, and potentially AI.**

##### 

##### **However, detecting real exoplanets accurately is a substantial scientific task, so you'd want to scope the project carefully.**
